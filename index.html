About

Web apps by day, debuggers by night.

I'm a fullstack developer who is comfortable on both the API and the UI side. I completed a six-month fullstack internship working in Node.js and TypeScript.

Outside of web work I do reverse engineering: reading process memory, hooking runtimes and taking game clients apart. I also care about security, and most of my repos start as experiments.
